Getting started

KejaLink connects people looking for a home with licensed, verified agents. Create an account to save homes, ask questions and arrange viewings.

Clients can search, save and book for free. Agents can list properties after their licence is verified by an administrator.

Create an account or log in to get started.

Searching for property

  1. Open Browse homes and filter by location, listing type, property type, price range and bedrooms.
  2. Choose how to sort results. Featured and newer listings appear first.
  3. Open a listing to view details, contact the agent or book a viewing.
  4. Log in with a client account to save a property to your favourites.

Booking a viewing

  1. Open an available listing and choose a date from tomorrow up to 30 days ahead.
  2. Choose one of the free times returned by the server, then request the viewing.
  3. Track the request and its status under My viewings after you log in.

Viewings are available Monday to Saturday. A time may be taken by another client before your request reaches the server.

Asking an agent a question

On a listing, enter a question of at least 10 characters and select Send inquiry. The agent's reply appears with the listing and in your inquiries.

Frequently asked questions

Is KejaLink free for clients?

Yes. Searching, saving, asking and booking are free.

Why can't I book a viewing?

You need a client account, and the property must be available and accepting viewings.

Why was my time slot rejected?

Another client may have booked it just before you. Select a different free time.

I registered as an agent but cannot add listings.

An administrator must verify your licence before you can publish listings.

How do I report a fake listing?

Open the listing and use Report this listing. Administrators review reports.

Error messages

Incorrect email or password.
Check your spelling and try again. Repeated failed attempts may temporarily lock the account.
Sorry, that time slot was just taken.
Choose another available time and submit your request again.
Request blocked for security reasons.
Refresh the page and try again.
Cannot reach the server.
Check your internet connection and try again.

Terms of use & privacy

  • Agents must hold a valid EARB licence and may only list properties they are authorised to market.
  • Listings must be accurate. Misleading listings may be removed.
  • Never pay a viewing fee or deposit before seeing a property in person. Report agents who ask.
  • We use your account information to provide KejaLink and share contact details with agents you contact or book with.
